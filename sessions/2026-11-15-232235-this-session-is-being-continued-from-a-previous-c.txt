  Why it's slow

  1. The 13 searches run one at a time. discover() waits for each Tavily call to
     finish before starting the next. Each call uses search_depth="advanced"
     and requests the full page text, so it takes several seconds. The total
     wait is the sum of all 13.
  2. There are 13 queries now, up from 8, and several overlap. Every extra query
     can return up to 5 more pages, and each page then goes through two Gemini
     calls: the source check and the extraction. Most of the collection time is
     probably spent in Gemini, not Tavily, so redundant queries cost time twice.
     The overlaps:
     - The four "head of" … queries mostly repeat "{name}" {domain} vice
       president OR "head of".
     - {domain} CEO CTO CFO COO CMO repeats the CEO/founder and CMO queries.
     - {domain} vice president VP SVP EVP and {domain} VP engineering product
       marketing sales have no company name in them. They tend to return loosely
       related pages that pass the cheap "mentions the domain" filter and then
       get thrown out by Gemini, after it has already spent time on them.
  3. The follow-up searches for weakly supported people also run one at a time.
     rag._corroborate does up to 10 web_search calls in sequence.

  Proposed changes, from biggest gain to smallest

  #: A
  Change: Run the discovery queries in parallel (ThreadPoolExecutor, about 5
    workers, the same pattern already used for classification and extraction),
    then dedupe the results in template order
  Expected effect: The search step takes about as long as its slowest few
  queries
    instead of all 13 added up
  Risk: None for results: the same pages come back, and the order stays
    deterministic
  ────────────────────────────────────────
  #: B
  Change: Cut the templates to about 9: drop the two domain-only VP queries and
    {domain} CEO CTO CFO COO CMO, and merge the four "head of" queries into two
    (engineering/product/technology and marketing/sales/finance/people)
  Expected effect: About 20 fewer candidate pages, so about 40 fewer Gemini
  calls
  Risk: Small; I'd check with a Campfire/Robinhood re-collection that nobody is
    lost
  ────────────────────────────────────────
  #: C
  Change: Run the follow-up searches in _corroborate in parallel
  Expected effect: Up to 10 sequential searches become about 2 rounds
  Risk: None
  ────────────────────────────────────────
  #: D
  Change: Optional: use "basic" depth for the broad whole-web queries and keep
    "advanced" for the site and LinkedIn queries
  Expected effect: Faster Tavily calls at half the credits
  Risk: Could miss some pages; I'd only do this if A–C aren't enough
